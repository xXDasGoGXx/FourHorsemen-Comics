You never know what you've got!

We buy all comics. Keys are our specialty.

We'll look at anything with staples, whether it's one issue or a lifetime of long boxes, and we treat every collection with the same care.

What we love most are the books that matter to collectors: first appearances, origins, classic covers and sharp Silver and Bronze Age copies. Plenty of those turn up in collections their owners called "just some old comics." Others turn out to be great reading copies, and those find good homes too.

Not sure what you've got? That's normal, and it's exactly why we're here. Send a few photos and we'll tell you what we see, with no cost and no pressure.

Selling to us

One book or a whole collection. Three steps, no haggling.

  1. Step 1

    Send photos

    Front and back covers of the books you're curious about, or a few shots of the whole box. A phone camera is fine.

  2. Step 2

    Get a straight answer

    We tell you what we see and make an offer based on what the same books are actually selling for right now.

  3. Step 3

    Your call

    Like the offer? We'll arrange payment and pickup or shipping. Not for you? No hard feelings, and you've learned what you have.

Why everyone wins

A fair deal is one you'd happily make again. That's the only kind we want.

If you're selling

  • Offers you can check against real, recent sales
  • One book or a whole collection, keys and readers alike
  • No consignment fees and no waiting for a buyer
  • A straight answer on what you have, even if we pass

If you're buying

  • Every book graded in hand, with flaws photographed and listed
  • CGC-certified copies and honestly graded raw copies
  • Prices in line with what the issue actually sells for
  • Want lists welcome: tell us what you're hunting

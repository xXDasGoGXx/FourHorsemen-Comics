Before you sell anywhere

  • Don't fix anything. No tape, no cleaning, no pressing out creases. Repairs usually hurt value.
  • Keep them flat and dry. Out of the sun and the garage heat, standing upright in a box.
  • Leave old bags alone if the books are fragile. Handling does more damage than an old bag.
  • A list beats a guess. Title, issue number and year off the cover is plenty to start.

Why there's no price list here

Comic prices move. A book that brought $1,000 last year might bring $300 today, or the other way around, and two copies of the same issue can be worlds apart because of condition. A printed price list would mislead you, so I give you a real number once I've seen the book.

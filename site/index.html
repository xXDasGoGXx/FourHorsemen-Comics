Selling to us

Single books, runs or a whole collection from 1961 to 1979. Three steps, no haggling.

  1. Step 1

    Send photos

    Front and back cover of each book, plus a list if you have one. A phone camera is fine.

  2. Step 2

    Get a written offer

    Each book is priced against recent sales of the same issue in the same grade, and we show you those sales.

  3. Step 3

    Get paid

    Say yes and you're paid the agreed amount, in person or on arrival of an insured shipment. Say no and nothing's owed.

Why it works for both of us

A fair deal is one you'd make again. That's the only kind we want.

If you're selling

  • Offers you can check against real, recent sales
  • One book or a whole collection, keys and readers alike
  • No consignment fees and no waiting for a buyer
  • A straight answer on books we don't buy

If you're buying

  • Every book graded in hand, with flaws photographed and listed
  • CGC-certified copies and honestly graded raw copies
  • Prices in line with what the issue actually sells for
  • Want lists welcome: tell us what you're hunting

How we grade

We use the standard 10-point scale. Tap a grade to see what it means for a book from the '60s or '70s.

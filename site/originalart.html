For the artist

How selling here would work

The art stays with you until it sells. Matthew runs the page and the payment; you ship and get paid.

  1. You

    Send photos and prices

    A straight-on photo of each piece, plus size, medium, year and your asking price.

  2. Matthew

    Pieces go live

    He adds them to this page and tells his local buyers and want-list customers.

  3. Buyer

    Pays through PayPal

    The money lands in Matthew's PayPal. He texts you the buyer's name and address right away.

  4. You

    Ship and send tracking

    Pack it flat, ship it insured, and text Matthew the tracking number.

  5. Matthew

    You get paid

    He adds the tracking to PayPal, then sends your share, within a few days of the sale.

The fee comes off the top before the split, so both of you share it. Example numbers only: the real cut and fee split are whatever you two agree. Buyer pays shipping on top.

What to agree on first

  • The cut. Consignment usually runs 15 to 30%.
  • Fees. Shared off the top (as in the example), or one side pays them.
  • Shipping. The buyer pays it; the artist packs and ships insured within an agreed number of days.
  • Selling elsewhere. If a piece sells somewhere else, tell Matthew the same day so it comes down.
  • Returns and damage. What happens if a piece arrives damaged or the buyer isn't happy.
  • Price changes. Who can lower a price, and whether offers are allowed.

Put it in a short written note you both keep. It protects both of you.

Price guide

What key Silver and Bronze Age books are really selling for: 50 recent auction results for CGC-graded copies, with the grade, the date and where each one sold.

Every figure is a real sale of a CGC-graded copy at Heritage Auctions (2024–26), including the buyer's premium. Where a book sold more than once in the same grade, we show the average. These are market results, not our own sales, and a different grade can mean a very different price. Each book links to its Heritage Auctions page, with full photos of that exact copy.
